## Motion contract and built implementation

The focal moment is a continually travelling 3D image field surrounding a quiet readable center. Forty-two tile instances comprise twenty placements, twenty half-cycle companions offset by 75 viewport-width units, and two desktop-only middle-edge anchors. Ten instances remain eligible below 700px. Travelling tiles drift horizontally, gently bob and turn independently around X/Y/Z, with perspective and individual scale. The two anchors stay at the middle edges, bobbing and turning without horizontal travel so those edges remain populated. Eleven UI8 product previews are retained; provenance/creator names/CDN sources and unresolved public reuse permission remain in hero-ui8-thumbnails.md. Original concept image remains network-error fallback only. This remains a local preview.

CSS-composited Web Animations API keyframes perform the continuous transforms without per-frame React state or DOM style writes. Existing Framer Motion transforms handle scroll dispersal and the flower handoff. Deterministic complete cycles last 220–280 seconds; rotations/bobbing are integer subcycles. Travelling positions wrap from −35 to below 115 across a 150-unit span. At each travelling tile's wrap point, two adjacent keyframes recycle it outside both viewport edges. Half-cycle companions keep travelling rows populated while their partners cross the center mask. A wider center-clearing mask below 700px protects stacked copy/actions. No dependency or playback controls added.

IntersectionObserver, page visibility and existing scroll progress pause the loops offscreen, in a hidden document or once gallery progress reaches .46. Mobile-hidden tiles remain paused, and matchMedia resumes them at desktop widths. Effects cancel animations and remove all listeners/observers on cleanup. Reduced motion creates no looping animations and retains stationary artwork plus the existing normal-flow fully open flower.
