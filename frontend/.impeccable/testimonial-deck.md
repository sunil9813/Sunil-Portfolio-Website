# Focused review deck

Local Testimonial redesign, Persuade. Preserve homepage identity, live API, compact controls and original section background. Rejected journal is an anti-reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Customer feedback is a browsable deck with one clear focal story.

OWN-WORLD: Existing charcoal canvas, understated slate card surfaces, white quotations and cyan interaction accents; Poppins and 40px pills.

STORY: Read the central customer, browse adjacent voices, expand complete feedback.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered heading and small live summary above a wide central review card. Smaller neighboring cards peek from either side. Navigation and customer selectors sit directly below.

FORM: Code-led local redesign, no identity seed. Spring-assisted manual card transitions, horizontal swipe, expanding full feedback; reduced motion and single-record fallback. No autoplay.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance. Component-scoped documentation only; no new rasters.
