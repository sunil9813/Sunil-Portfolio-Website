## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

The existing portfolio presents Sunil B.K's MERN development work, projects, courses, notes, blog content and customer feedback. Visitors can explore work and contact Sunil about a project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React, Node.js/Express and MongoDB are the stack named on the homepage. Customer feedback is loaded from the existing public testimonial service. Fictional demo reviews are explicitly labeled and excluded from real customer rating averages. Reviewer photos must come from their uploaded avatar records; missing photos use initials, not unrelated portraits. Scoped frontend work must preserve unrelated user edits and support keyboard and reduced-motion users.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the original dark homepage background, existing Poppins typography and compact action sizing. Specific reference layouts and per-review colors are surface decisions, not a homepage-wide theme change.

## Frontend Implementation Preference

Use Tailwind CSS first for newly built and edited UI, including migrating the existing styles of a component being refined. Use SCSS only for styling that Tailwind cannot reasonably express. This preference does not authorize unrelated, site-wide rewrites.
